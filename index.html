Rabbi Dov Brezak

Rabbi Dov Brezak is a leading figure in Jewish education, bringing decades of experience as a parent, principal, author, lecturer and consultant. He addresses the real challenges facing parents and children today with practical techniques grounded in Torah values.

He founded Chinuch LifeLines to make this guidance available to every family, with trained parenting coaches ready to help parents around the world.

Author of the bestseller Chinuch in Turbulent Times (ArtScroll).
