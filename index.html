About

Rabbi Dov Brezak

A leading voice in Jewish education, with decades of experience as a parent, principal, author, lecturer and consultant. His bestseller Chinuch in Turbulent Times (ArtScroll) is read by parents around the world.

He founded Chinuch LifeLines so that parents everywhere can get practical, encouraging guidance rooted in Torah values and love for every child.
