Questions

Good to know

The timing doesn't work for me. Can I get recordings?

Yes! All classes are recorded, and you can sign up to receive the mp3 by email after each class.

Can I listen on the phone?

Yes. All classes are stored on our phone line system, which you can call into anytime.

How does payment work?

Each workshop costs $47 a month. You are charged on the date you sign up, and the charge recurs monthly unless it is cancelled.

Can I cancel anytime?

There is a 3-month commitment from when you sign up. After that, you may cancel at any time.
